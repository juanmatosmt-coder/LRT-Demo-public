SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method measures the hardness of metal parts and plating with a microhardness tester (SYN-AN-HARD), usually on a polished cross-section.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must say where to indent: the location and the surface (for example the spring arm, on a polished cross-section or on the surface). It should give the scale and load (for example HV 0.1), the number of indents per sample, and the specification if the result is to be judged.

## Sample quantity
Test at least 3 samples per configuration. Fewer samples need a reason in the request.

## Sample preparation
Indents are made on a flat, polished surface. Thin parts are cross-sectioned first (SYN-AN-152); the indent must be at least two indent widths from an edge.

## Test sequence
1. Check the tester on a hardness block. 2. Make the indents at the stated locations. 3. Measure the diagonals. 4. Calculate the hardness.

## Reporting
Report each indent value, the average and range, the scale and load, the location of each indent, and whether the values meet the specification.
