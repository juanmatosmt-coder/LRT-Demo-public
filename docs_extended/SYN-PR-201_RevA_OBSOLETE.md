## Applicability
Applies to samples submitted for warpage measurement.

## Moisture preconditioning
Bake samples at 105 C for 8 hours. Samples may be stored in room air for up to one week after baking.

## Handling
Handle samples by the edges. Gloves are recommended.

## Information required from the requester
The request should state the housing material if known.
