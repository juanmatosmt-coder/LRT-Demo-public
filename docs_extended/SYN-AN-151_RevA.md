SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method identifies polymers, resins and organic residues by infrared spectroscopy with an attenuated total reflection (ATR) accessory (SYN-AN-FTIR), and compares spectra with a reference or a library.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must say what is to be identified (the bulk material, a surface residue or contamination, or a difference between two lots) and where the sample is taken: which part and which area or feature, marked on a drawing or photograph attached to the request. A reference (a known-good part, resin pellets, or the expected material and its datasheet) should be supplied when possible.

## Sample quantity
There is no minimum. One part per material or lot is usually enough; send the reference with it.

## Sample preparation
Residues are collected from the marked area without solvents unless the request allows them. Bulk material is cut from the stated area, away from surfaces that may carry mould release.

## Test sequence
1. Record the background. 2. Measure the reference, if supplied. 3. Measure the sample at the marked area. 4. Compare with the reference and the library.

## Reporting
Report the spectra, the best library matches with their match quality, the comparison with the reference, where each spectrum was taken, and the identification with its confidence.
