SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method measures plating layer thicknesses with an X-ray fluorescence instrument (SYN-AN-XRF), without damaging the part.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must give the layers and their specification (for example gold 0.76 µm minimum over nickel 1.27 µm minimum) and where to measure: which spots on the contact (for example the contact area and the tail) and how many points per sample. It should say whether the results are judged against the specification or only reported.

## Sample quantity
Measure at least 3 samples per configuration. Fewer samples need a reason in the request.

## Sample preparation
Samples are measured as received. The measured spot must be flat enough for the instrument's collimator; very small or curved areas are reported as approximate.

## Test sequence
1. Check the instrument against a thickness standard. 2. Measure each requested spot. 3. Repeat for each sample.

## Reporting
Report each layer thickness per spot and sample, the average and the minimum, the collimator size, and the spots below the specification.
