SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method cuts, mounts, grinds and polishes a sample to show an internal plane, then inspects and measures it under an optical microscope (SYN-AN-METALLO): plating layers, solder joints, crimp compaction, cracks and voids.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must say where to section, as a location (a contact, joint or feature) and a plane (for example through position 5, parallel to the mating axis), ideally on a marked drawing or photograph attached to the request, and what is to be measured or inspected on the section. It should give the number of sections per sample and whether the samples may be potted.

## Sample quantity
There is no minimum. One section per sample is usual; more sections need more time.

## Sample preparation
Samples are potted in resin unless the request forbids it, then cut close to the plane and ground to it. The test is destructive.

## Test sequence
1. Photograph the sample and mark the plane. 2. Pot the sample. 3. Cut and grind to the plane. 4. Polish and, if needed, etch. 5. Inspect and measure.

## Reporting
Report images of each section with scale bars, where each plane lies on the part, the measurements requested, and any defects seen.
