SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method mates and unmates connector pairs a set number of times on a mating cycle fixture (SYN-MECH-CYCLER, up to 12 cycles per minute) to show wear. It is an exposure: the result is the change in readouts such as LLCR (SYN-TM-130) or mating force (SYN-TM-140) taken before and after.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must give the number of mating cycles and the rate, whether the mating half is supplied (part number) and how both halves are held and aligned, and what is measured before, during and after cycling. Acceptance limits after cycling (for example resistance change, force change, no wear through the plating) should be given if the result is to be judged.

## Sample quantity
Cycle at least 5 mated pairs per configuration. Fewer samples need a reason in the request.

## Sample preparation
Both halves are mounted as in use, on test boards or in fixtures that keep them aligned so that cycling does not side-load the contacts. Samples are not cleaned or lubricated unless the request says so.

## Test sequence
1. Take the initial readouts. 2. Cycle at the requested rate. 3. Stop at each requested count for intermediate readouts. 4. Take the final readouts. 5. Inspect the contact areas for wear.

## Reporting
Report the readouts at each count and their change from the initial values, the cycle rate, the fixture, and photographs of the contact areas after cycling.
