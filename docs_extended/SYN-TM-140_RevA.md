SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method measures forces on connectors and contacts with a universal force test frame (SYN-MECH-UTM, load cells up to 1000 N): mating and unmating (insertion and withdrawal) force, contact or terminal retention in the housing, crimp pull-out, and contact normal force. Durability cycling is a separate method (SYN-TM-142).

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must name the force test (mating and unmating, retention, crimp pull-out or normal force) and say where and how the force is applied: the axis, how the sample is held (fixture, test board or clamp), and whether the mating half is supplied, with its part number. It should give the test speed in mm/min and the end point (full mating, a set distance, or until separation or failure), and the acceptance limits in newtons if the result is to be judged.

## Sample quantity
Test at least 5 samples per configuration. Fewer samples need a reason in the request.

## Sample preparation
Samples are tested as received unless the request names a conditioning. Each sample is held in the same orientation; the fixture must not load the contact anywhere except along the stated axis. Retention and crimp pull-out tests are destructive.

## Test sequence
1. Zero the load cell with the fixture fitted. 2. Mount the sample and align it with the stated axis. 3. Run at the requested speed to the end point. 4. Record the force-displacement curve and the peak force. 5. Repeat for each sample and position.

## Reporting
Report the peak force for each sample, the force-displacement curves, the speed, the fixture and the orientation, and any sample outside the limits.
