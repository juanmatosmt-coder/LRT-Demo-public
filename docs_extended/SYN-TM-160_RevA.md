SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method shows pores in noble-metal plating (gold or palladium over nickel) by exposing samples to an acid vapor that corrodes the base metal through the pores (SYN-AN-PORO). Corrosion spots on the evaluated surface are counted.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must give the plating layers and their minimum thicknesses, and which surfaces are evaluated (for example the contact area within 1 mm of the mating point), as defined on the drawing. It should name the method if it is not the default acid vapor exposure, and the acceptance limit as a pore count per area.

## Sample quantity
Test at least 3 samples per configuration. Fewer samples need a reason in the request.

## Sample preparation
Samples are unmated and not handled on the evaluated surfaces. They are not cleaned unless the request says so. The test is destructive.

## Test sequence
1. Inspect the evaluated surfaces before exposure. 2. Expose to the acid vapor for the method's time. 3. Dry the samples. 4. Count corrosion spots on the evaluated surfaces under the microscope.

## Reporting
Report the pore count for each sample and surface, photographs, the method and exposure time, and the samples outside the limit.
