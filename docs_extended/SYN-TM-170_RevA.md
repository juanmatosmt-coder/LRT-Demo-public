SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method checks that insulation between contacts, and between contacts and the shell, withstands a test voltage without breakdown (DWV) and measures its insulation resistance (IR), on the lab's high-voltage tester (SYN-ELEC-HIPOT, up to 3 kV).

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must give the test voltage (AC or DC) and its duration, and between which points it is applied: adjacent contacts, every contact to the shell, or contacts to ground, with the positions. It should give the limits (maximum leakage current, minimum insulation resistance) and the sample condition: mated or unmated, and before or after an exposure such as humidity.

## Sample quantity
Test at least 3 samples per configuration. Fewer samples need a reason in the request.

## Sample preparation
Samples are clean and dry unless the test follows a humidity exposure, in which case the request gives the time between exposure and test.

## Test sequence
1. Measure insulation resistance at the stated DC voltage. 2. Ramp to the DWV test voltage. 3. Hold for the stated time while monitoring leakage. 4. Ramp down. 5. Repeat for each pair of points.

## Reporting
Report insulation resistance and leakage current for each pair of points, any breakdown or flashover, the voltages, durations and sample condition.
