SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
Applies when a requester asks for samples to be cleaned or marked by the lab before testing. It does not apply to samples tested as received.

## Cleaning
Clean samples with isopropyl alcohol and a lint-free wipe. Do not use ultrasonic cleaning on housings with inserted contacts.

## Drying
Air-dry cleaned samples for at least 30 minutes before marking or measurement.

## Marking
Mark sample IDs with a fine-tip marker on a surface outside the measurement area. Never mark the seating plane or the mating face.

## Records
Record the cleaning agent, date and operator on the preparation log.
