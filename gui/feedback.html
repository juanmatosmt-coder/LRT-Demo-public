Feedback

What engineers, technicians and lab reviewers say the assistant should ask and check. Kept on this server only.

How to give it: on the Request forms page, under an answer or the Check quality list, press Give feedback on this: rate items Useful, Not needed or Wrong, or Suggest a missing question or check.

How it becomes a rule (feedback never changes the assistant by itself):

  1. Read new entries; set Accepted or Rejected with a note.
  2. A person adds accepted items to the procedure, the assistant's questions (assistant/guardrails.py) or the quality check (assistant/quality.py).
  3. Set Added, naming where it went.
