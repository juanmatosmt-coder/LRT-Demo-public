SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Scope
This method measures coplanarity of connector housings at 25 C only. It does not apply to measurements during heating.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request must include the measurement area, the datum, and the acceptance limit. No thermal profile is required.

## Sample quantity
A minimum of 10 samples per lot is required.

## Test sequence
1. Stabilize samples at 25 C for at least 2 hours. 2. Measure coplanarity. 3. Report per the requester's format.
