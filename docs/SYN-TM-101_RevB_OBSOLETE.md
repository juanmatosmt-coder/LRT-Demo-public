## Scope
This method measures warpage of connector housings during a reflow-simulation thermal profile using shadow moire.

## Required inputs before scheduling
The request should include the peak temperature of the reflow profile and the acceptance limit, if one applies.

## Sample preparation
Moisture preconditioning before warpage measurement is optional and is performed only at the requester's discretion.

## Sample quantity
A minimum of 3 samples per test condition is required.

## Test sequence
1. Measure each sample at 25 C. 2. Heat to peak temperature and capture data. 3. Cool to room temperature.
