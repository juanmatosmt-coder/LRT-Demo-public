SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - created for a course demonstration. Not a company procedure.

## Applicability
Applies to samples submitted for any elevated-temperature test, including warpage during reflow simulation and thermal cycling exposure.

## Moisture preconditioning
Bake samples at 125 C for 24 hours. Begin the test within 8 hours of removal from the oven, or store samples in a dry cabinet below 10 percent relative humidity for no more than 72 hours. If the supplier provides documented moisture-sensitivity handling for the parts, the requester may cite that document instead, and the lab records it with the results.

## Handling
Wear gloves. Do not touch the measurement surface. Keep samples in labeled trays during transfer.

## Information required from the requester
The request must state the sample storage history since molding, whether the samples are as-molded or taken from a reflowed assembly, and the housing material grade if known.

## Traceability
Record sample IDs, bake start and end times, and oven ID on the preparation log attached to the request.
